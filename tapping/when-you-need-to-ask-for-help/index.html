EFT tapping script: When You Need to Ask for Help

Use this session when you need practical or emotional support but fear of being a burden, appearing incapable, or hearing no is stopping you from asking.

Tap gently with two fingers, about seven times on each point. Say each phrase out loud or quietly in your head. There's no perfect way to do it.

Round 1: Say what's true

  1. Side of handI need help, and I do not want to ask.
  2. Top of headI am afraid of becoming a burden.
  3. EyebrowPart of me believes I should handle this alone.
  4. Side of eyeI keep minimizing how overwhelmed I feel.
  5. Under eyeMy body tenses when I imagine making the request.
  6. Under noseI worry they will think I am incapable.
  7. ChinI am afraid there will not be room for what I need.
  8. CollarboneI can admit that asking feels vulnerable.
  9. Under armTrying to do everything alone is exhausting me.

Take one easy breath. Notice whether anything has shifted, even slightly. If nothing has changed yet, that is completely okay. Keep breathing, and we will continue.

Round 2: Soften it

  1. Side of handI can make one clear request.
  2. Top of headNeeding support is part of being human.
  3. EyebrowAsking gives the other person a choice.
  4. Side of eyeI can be specific about what would help.
  5. Under eyeMy body can soften before I reach out.
  6. Under noseA request does not make me incapable.
  7. ChinA no would not mean my need is unreasonable.
  8. CollarboneI can choose someone who may have capacity.
  9. Under armI can let support become possible.

Take another slow breath. Notice what feels different now. You do not need the feeling to disappear. See if there is simply a little less pressure, or a little more choice in how you meet this moment.

Round 3: Choose what's next

  1. Side of handI am ready to ask for the help I need.
  2. Top of headI can speak honestly without overexplaining.
  3. EyebrowI trust myself to make a respectful request.
  4. Side of eyeI can receive support without apologizing for existing.
  5. Under eyeMy body can stay grounded while I ask.
  6. Under noseI am still capable when I collaborate.
  7. ChinI can adjust if this person is not available.
  8. CollarboneConnection can make difficult things more manageable.
  9. Under armI am ready to reach out.
