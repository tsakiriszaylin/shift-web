EFT tapping script: Waking Up From a Bad Dream

Use this session right after waking from a bad dream, when your body still feels the fear even though it wasn't real.

Tap gently with two fingers, about seven times on each point. Say each phrase out loud or quietly in your head. There's no perfect way to do it.

Round 1: Say what's true

  1. Side of handThat dream shook me and I’m still feeling it.
  2. Top of headMy heart is racing like it was real.
  3. EyebrowPart of me isn’t sure I’m safe yet.
  4. Side of eyeI notice how tense my whole body feels.
  5. Under eyeMy mind keeps replaying pieces of the dream.
  6. Under noseI feel shaken, even though I’m awake now.
  7. ChinFalling back asleep feels scary right now.
  8. CollarboneI can admit that dream really scared me.
  9. Under armThis fear is lingering, even though the dream is over.

Take one easy breath. Notice whether anything has shifted, even slightly. If nothing has changed yet, that’s completely okay. Keep breathing, and we’ll continue.

Round 2: Soften it

  1. Side of handI can remind myself that I’m safe right now.
  2. Top of headThat was a dream, and I’m here in my own bed.
  3. EyebrowI don’t have to carry that fear into this moment.
  4. Side of eyeI can feel my body starting to settle.
  5. Under eyeMy mind can let that dream fade now.
  6. Under noseI’m allowed to feel shaken and still be okay.
  7. ChinI can breathe slowly until my heart catches up.
  8. CollarboneI’m allowed to change position or open my eyes if it helps.
  9. Under armThis room is quiet, and I’m safe in it.

Take another slow breath. Notice what feels different now. You don’t need the feeling to disappear. See if there’s simply a little less pressure, or a little more choice in how you meet this moment.

Round 3: Choose what's next

  1. Side of handI’m capable of calming myself back down.
  2. Top of headI trust that I’m safe here, right now.
  3. EyebrowI’m allowed to fall back asleep peacefully.
  4. Side of eyeI can let my body feel heavy and calm again.
  5. Under eyeMy mind can rest now that I’m grounded.
  6. Under noseI’m learning that I can settle myself after moments like this.
  7. ChinI can trust the quiet of this room.
  8. CollarboneI’m allowed to feel safe enough to close my eyes again.
  9. Under armI’m ready to drift back into easy, restful sleep.
