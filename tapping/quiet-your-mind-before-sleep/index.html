EFT tapping script: Quiet Your Mind Before Sleep

Use this session in bed when your thoughts keep jumping between tomorrow, today, and everything you cannot solve right now.

Tap gently with two fingers, about seven times on each point. Say each phrase out loud or quietly in your head. There's no perfect way to do it.

Round 1: Say what's true

  1. Side of handMy mind does not want to switch off.
  2. Top of headI keep thinking about tomorrow.
  3. EyebrowI am replaying what happened today.
  4. Side of eyeEvery thought seems to lead to another.
  5. Under eyeMy body is tired but my mind is alert.
  6. Under noseI am worried I will not get enough sleep.
  7. ChinTrying to force sleep is creating more pressure.
  8. CollarboneI can admit I feel restless.
  9. Under armThere is so much noise in my head.

Take one easy breath. Notice whether anything has shifted, even slightly. If nothing has changed yet, that’s completely okay. Keep breathing, and we’ll continue.

Round 2: Soften it

  1. Side of handI do not have to solve tomorrow tonight.
  2. Top of headThese thoughts can wait until morning.
  3. EyebrowI can let one thought pass without following it.
  4. Side of eyeI can return to the feeling of the bed.
  5. Under eyeMy body is allowed to grow heavy.
  6. Under noseRest still counts even before sleep comes.
  7. ChinI can release the pressure to fall asleep.
  8. CollarboneI can let the night hold me.
  9. Under armI can make room for quiet.

Take another slow breath. Notice what feels different now. You do not need the feeling to disappear. See if there is simply a little less pressure, or a little more choice in how you meet this moment.

Round 3: Choose what's next

  1. Side of handI am safe to rest now.
  2. Top of headTomorrow will meet me when it arrives.
  3. EyebrowI can let today be complete.
  4. Side of eyeMy thoughts can drift without needing answers.
  5. Under eyeMy body remembers how to sleep.
  6. Under noseI welcome a slower breath.
  7. ChinI do not need to monitor the clock.
  8. CollarboneI can trust rest to come in its own time.
  9. Under armI am ready to let go.
