EFT tapping script: Something for When You're Bored

Use this session when boredom feels restless or uncomfortable and you're tempted to fill it with anything just to feel occupied.

Tap gently with two fingers, about seven times on each point. Say each phrase out loud or quietly in your head. There's no perfect way to do it.

Round 1: Say what's true

  1. Side of handI feel bored and restless right now.
  2. Top of headI keep reaching for something to fill this moment.
  3. EyebrowThis stillness feels uncomfortable.
  4. Side of eyeI want to scroll, snack, or do anything but sit here.
  5. Under eyeMy body feels antsy and unsettled.
  6. Under nosePart of me thinks boredom means something is wrong.
  7. ChinI can admit this restlessness is real.
  8. CollarboneI notice how badly I want to escape this feeling.
  9. Under armSitting with nothing to do feels harder than it should.

Take one easy breath. Notice whether anything has shifted, even slightly. If nothing has changed yet, that’s completely okay. Keep breathing, and we’ll continue.

Round 2: Soften it

  1. Side of handI can let this restlessness be here without acting on it.
  2. Top of headI don’t have to fill every quiet moment.
  3. EyebrowThis boredom isn’t an emergency.
  4. Side of eyeI can let my mind wander instead of reaching for my phone.
  5. Under eyeMy body can settle even without something to do.
  6. Under noseI’m allowed to just be, without a reason.
  7. ChinThis stillness can hold something too.
  8. CollarboneI can notice the urge to distract myself without following it.
  9. Under armI can let this moment pass at its own pace.

Take another slow breath. Notice what feels different now. You don’t need the feeling to disappear. See if there’s simply a little less pressure, or a little more choice in how you meet this moment.

Round 3: Choose what's next

  1. Side of handI’m capable of sitting with stillness.
  2. Top of headI trust that this restlessness will pass on its own.
  3. EyebrowI’m allowed to do nothing for a while.
  4. Side of eyeI can find something quietly interesting in this moment.
  5. Under eyeMy body can relax into having nowhere to be.
  6. Under noseI’m learning that boredom isn’t dangerous.
  7. ChinI can let this time be simple.
  8. CollarboneI’m allowed to rest without earning it.
  9. Under armI’m ready to let this moment be enough.
