EFT tapping script: When You’re Second-Guessing a Big Decision

Use this session when you have a meaningful decision to make and repeatedly reviewing every possibility is leaving you more anxious instead of more certain.

Tap gently with two fingers, about seven times on each point. Say each phrase out loud or quietly in your head. There's no perfect way to do it.

Round 1: Say what's true

  1. Side of handI keep second-guessing this decision.
  2. Top of headI am afraid of choosing the wrong path.
  3. EyebrowMy mind keeps reopening every possibility.
  4. Side of eyeI want a guarantee before I decide.
  5. Under eyeMy body feels caught between competing options.
  6. Under noseI feel responsible for predicting the entire future.
  7. ChinPart of me believes more thinking will create certainty.
  8. CollarboneI can admit that I feel stuck.
  9. Under armThis decision is taking up so much space.

Take one easy breath. Notice whether anything has shifted, even slightly. If nothing has changed yet, that is completely okay. Keep breathing, and we will continue.

Round 2: Soften it

  1. Side of handI can pause the debate for one moment.
  2. Top of headNo meaningful choice comes with complete certainty.
  3. EyebrowI can separate real information from imagined outcomes.
  4. Side of eyeI do not have to solve every future consequence today.
  5. Under eyeMy body can soften while the question remains open.
  6. Under noseI can notice which values matter most here.
  7. ChinMore thinking is not always more clarity.
  8. CollarboneI can give myself permission to choose imperfectly.
  9. Under armI can let the decision become more manageable.

Take another slow breath. Notice what feels different now. You do not need the feeling to disappear. See if there is simply a little less pressure, or a little more choice in how you meet this moment.

Round 3: Choose what's next

  1. Side of handI trust myself to make a thoughtful choice.
  2. Top of headI can act on the best information I have now.
  3. EyebrowI know what matters most to me.
  4. Side of eyeI can adjust if new information appears.
  5. Under eyeMy body can feel grounded as I decide.
  6. Under noseI do not need certainty to move forward.
  7. ChinI can choose without abandoning myself.
  8. CollarboneI trust my ability to meet what comes next.
  9. Under armI am ready for one clear next step.
