EFT tapping script: Starting a New Job and Feeling Out of Place

Use this session when a new job, team, or role has left you feeling uncertain, behind, or worried that you do not belong.

Tap gently with two fingers, about seven times on each point. Say each phrase out loud or quietly in your head. There's no perfect way to do it.

Round 1: Say what's true

  1. Side of handI want to belong here, but I feel out of place.
  2. Top of headEveryone else seems to know what they are doing.
  3. EyebrowI am worried people can see my uncertainty.
  4. Side of eyeI keep comparing my beginning to their experience.
  5. Under eyeMy body feels tense and overly alert.
  6. Under noseI feel pressure to prove myself quickly.
  7. ChinPart of me is afraid I made the wrong choice.
  8. CollarboneI can admit that being new feels vulnerable.
  9. Under armI am carrying more pressure than this moment requires.

Take one easy breath. Notice whether anything has shifted, even slightly. If nothing has changed yet, that is completely okay. Keep breathing, and we will continue.

Round 2: Soften it

  1. Side of handI am allowed to be new here.
  2. Top of headI do not need to know everything on day one.
  3. EyebrowQuestions are part of learning, not proof of failure.
  4. Side of eyeI can let other people's experience guide me.
  5. Under eyeMy body can soften as I find my rhythm.
  6. Under noseI can focus on learning instead of performing.
  7. ChinUncertainty does not mean I do not belong.
  8. CollarboneI can notice one thing that is becoming familiar.
  9. Under armI can give myself time to settle in.

Take another slow breath. Notice what feels different now. You do not need the feeling to disappear. See if there is simply a little less pressure, or a little more choice in how you meet this moment.

Round 3: Choose what's next

  1. Side of handI bring value even while I am learning.
  2. Top of headI can build confidence through small experiences.
  3. EyebrowI trust myself to ask thoughtful questions.
  4. Side of eyeI can connect with one person at a time.
  5. Under eyeMy body can feel grounded in this new place.
  6. Under noseI do not have to earn belonging through perfection.
  7. ChinI am capable of growing into this role.
  8. CollarboneI can meet today without mastering the whole job.
  9. Under armI am ready to take my place here.
