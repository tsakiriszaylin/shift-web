Free EFT tapping scripts for real-life moments

EFT tapping means gently tapping on points on your face and body while you name what you're feeling. These 84 scripts are written for specific moments, so you don't have to come up with the words yourself.

Each script uses the same nine points: Side of hand, Top of head, Eyebrow, Side of eye, Under eye, Under nose, Chin, Collarbone, Under arm.
