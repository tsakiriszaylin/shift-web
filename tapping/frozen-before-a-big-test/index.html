EFT tapping script: Frozen Before a Big Test

Use this session right before a test or exam when your mind has gone blank and pressure is making it hard to access what you've studied.

Tap gently with two fingers, about seven times on each point. Say each phrase out loud or quietly in your head. There's no perfect way to do it.

Round 1: Say what's true

  1. Side of handI am nervous about this test.
  2. Top of headMy mind feels like it is going blank.
  3. EyebrowI am afraid I will forget everything I studied.
  4. Side of eyeI keep imagining getting the answers wrong.
  5. Under eyeMy body feels tense before I have even started.
  6. Under noseI want to feel more prepared than I do.
  7. ChinPart of me believes this test decides everything.
  8. CollarboneI can admit that I feel overwhelmed.
  9. Under armThe pressure is making it hard to think clearly.

Take one easy breath. Notice whether anything has shifted, even slightly. If nothing has changed yet, that is completely okay. Keep breathing, and we will continue.

Round 2: Soften it

  1. Side of handI can let my mind settle before I begin.
  2. Top of headA blank moment does not mean I do not know the material.
  3. EyebrowI can trust the studying I already did.
  4. Side of eyeI do not have to picture every wrong answer.
  5. Under eyeMy body can release some of this tension.
  6. Under noseI can take one breath before I read the first question.
  7. ChinThis test is one moment, not my whole story.
  8. CollarboneI can support myself instead of pressuring myself.
  9. Under armI can meet this test one question at a time.

Take another slow breath. Notice what feels different now. You do not need the feeling to disappear. See if there is simply a little less pressure, or a little more choice in how you meet this moment.

Round 3: Choose what's next

  1. Side of handI am ready to show what I know.
  2. Top of headMy mind can clear enough to think.
  3. EyebrowI trust myself to recall what I have learned.
  4. Side of eyeI can focus on the question in front of me.
  5. Under eyeMy body can stay calm enough to concentrate.
  6. Under noseI can breathe and begin.
  7. ChinI do not need to be perfect, only present.
  8. CollarboneI am capable of working through this.
  9. Under armI am ready to begin.
