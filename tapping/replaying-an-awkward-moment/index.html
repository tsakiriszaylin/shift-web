EFT tapping script: Replaying an Awkward Moment

Use this session when something you said or did keeps replaying and embarrassment is making the moment feel bigger than it was.

Tap gently with two fingers, about seven times on each point. Say each phrase out loud or quietly in your head. There's no perfect way to do it.

Round 1: Say what's true

  1. Side of handI keep replaying that awkward moment.
  2. Top of headI wish I could take it back.
  3. EyebrowI am imagining what everyone thought.
  4. Side of eyeThe memory keeps making me cringe.
  5. Under eyeI can feel the embarrassment in my body.
  6. Under noseI am being so hard on myself.
  7. ChinPart of me thinks this says something about who I am.
  8. CollarboneI can admit I feel exposed.
  9. Under armThis small moment feels enormous right now.

Take one easy breath. Notice whether anything has shifted, even slightly. If nothing has changed yet, that’s completely okay. Keep breathing, and we’ll continue.

Round 2: Soften it

  1. Side of handI can let myself be human.
  2. Top of headMost people are thinking about their own lives.
  3. EyebrowI do not know what anyone else thought.
  4. Side of eyeThe replay is not protecting me.
  5. Under eyeMy body can release this embarrassment.
  6. Under noseI can speak to myself with kindness.
  7. ChinOne awkward moment does not define me.
  8. CollarboneI can learn without reliving it.
  9. Under armI can let the moment get smaller.

Take another slow breath. Notice what feels different now. You do not need the feeling to disappear. See if there is simply a little less pressure, or a little more choice in how you meet this moment.

Round 3: Choose what's next

  1. Side of handI am allowed to move on.
  2. Top of headI belong even when I am imperfect.
  3. EyebrowI can remember this with perspective.
  4. Side of eyeI choose the present over the replay.
  5. Under eyeMy body can feel safe again.
  6. Under noseI am still worthy of connection.
  7. ChinBeing human is not a failure.
  8. CollarboneI can trust this feeling to pass.
  9. Under armI am ready to return to my day.
