EFT tapping script: Regretting Something You Just Posted

Use this session right after posting something online and immediately regretting it, when anxiety about how it will be perceived takes over.

Tap gently with two fingers, about seven times on each point. Say each phrase out loud or quietly in your head. There's no perfect way to do it.

Round 1: Say what's true

  1. Side of handI just posted something and I’m already regretting it.
  2. Top of headMy mind keeps imagining how people will react.
  3. EyebrowI’m worried I revealed too much.
  4. Side of eyeI keep checking to see who has seen it.
  5. Under eyeMy body feels anxious waiting for a reaction.
  6. Under noseI want to take it back immediately.
  7. ChinPart of me feels embarrassed already.
  8. CollarboneI can admit that I feel exposed right now.
  9. Under armThis is taking up a lot of space in my mind.

Take one easy breath. Notice whether anything has shifted, even slightly. If nothing has changed yet, that’s completely okay. Keep breathing, and we’ll continue.

Round 2: Soften it

  1. Side of handI can let this post exist without controlling every reaction.
  2. Top of headPeople are far less focused on me than I imagine.
  3. EyebrowI can be honest online without it being a mistake.
  4. Side of eyeI don’t need to track every response.
  5. Under eyeMy body can settle while I wait.
  6. Under noseI can delete it or leave it, either is okay.
  7. ChinOne post doesn’t define how people see me.
  8. CollarboneI can trust that what I shared was genuine.
  9. Under armI can put my phone down for a while.

Take another slow breath. Notice what feels different now. You don’t need the feeling to disappear. See if there’s simply a little less pressure, or a little more choice in how you meet this moment.

Round 3: Choose what's next

  1. Side of handI trust myself to show up honestly online.
  2. Top of headMy mind can let this go.
  3. EyebrowI’m allowed to be imperfectly myself in public.
  4. Side of eyeI can focus on my actual life, not just reactions to it.
  5. Under eyeMy body can relax about this.
  6. Under noseI can handle whatever response comes.
  7. ChinI don’t need everyone's approval.
  8. CollarboneI’m someone who can be real, even online.
  9. Under armI’m ready to let this be finished.
