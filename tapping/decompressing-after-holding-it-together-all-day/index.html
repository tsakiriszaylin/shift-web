EFT tapping script: Decompressing After Holding It Together All Day

Use this session at the end of a day spent holding yourself together for other people, when you finally have space to let it go.

Tap gently with two fingers, about seven times on each point. Say each phrase out loud or quietly in your head. There's no perfect way to do it.

Round 1: Say what's true

  1. Side of handI have been holding it together all day.
  2. Top of headMy mind is finally allowed to slow down.
  3. EyebrowI have been managing everyone else's needs and reactions.
  4. Side of eyeI notice how much energy that took.
  5. Under eyeMy body feels worn out from staying composed.
  6. Under noseI want to stop performing and just exist.
  7. ChinPart of me has been holding back all day.
  8. CollarboneI can admit how tired I actually am.
  9. Under armIt has taken a lot to get through today.

Take one easy breath. Notice whether anything has shifted, even slightly. If nothing has changed yet, that is completely okay. Keep breathing, and we will continue.

Round 2: Soften it

  1. Side of handI can let this composure soften now.
  2. Top of headI do not have to perform anymore right now.
  3. EyebrowI can release what I was holding for other people.
  4. Side of eyeI am allowed to feel however I actually feel.
  5. Under eyeMy body can finally exhale.
  6. Under noseI can let today's effort be enough.
  7. ChinI do not owe anyone my composure right now.
  8. CollarboneI can take up this quiet space for myself.
  9. Under armI can let the day fall away.

Take another slow breath. Notice what feels different now. You do not need the feeling to disappear. See if there is simply a little less pressure, or a little more choice in how you meet this moment.

Round 3: Choose what's next

  1. Side of handI am allowed to fully unwind.
  2. Top of headMy mind can rest from managing everything.
  3. EyebrowI trust myself to show up again tomorrow.
  4. Side of eyeI can be completely myself right now.
  5. Under eyeMy body can finally settle.
  6. Under noseI did enough today.
  7. ChinI am allowed to just be, without holding anything up.
  8. CollarboneI can receive this rest fully.
  9. Under armI am ready to let go for tonight.
