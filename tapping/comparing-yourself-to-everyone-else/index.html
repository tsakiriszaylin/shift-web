EFT tapping script: Comparing Yourself to Everyone Else

Use this session when other people’s milestones, appearance, or success are making your own path feel inadequate.

Tap gently with two fingers, about seven times on each point. Say each phrase out loud or quietly in your head. There's no perfect way to do it.

Round 1: Say what's true

  1. Side of handI keep comparing myself to everyone else.
  2. Top of headTheir progress makes me feel behind.
  3. EyebrowI am focusing on everything they have that I do not.
  4. Side of eyeMy mind is turning their life into a measure of mine.
  5. Under eyeI can feel this inadequacy in my body.
  6. Under nosePart of me believes I should be further along.
  7. ChinI am overlooking how much I have carried.
  8. CollarboneI can admit that comparison is hurting me.
  9. Under armIt feels like everyone else is moving faster.

Take one easy breath. Notice whether anything has shifted, even slightly. If nothing has changed yet, that is completely okay. Keep breathing, and we will continue.

Round 2: Soften it

  1. Side of handI can notice comparison without following it.
  2. Top of headI am seeing a snapshot, not a whole life.
  3. EyebrowSomeone else’s progress does not erase mine.
  4. Side of eyeMy path is allowed to have a different rhythm.
  5. Under eyeMy body can soften around the feeling of being behind.
  6. Under noseI can remember what matters to me.
  7. ChinI do not need to turn inspiration into punishment.
  8. CollarboneI can acknowledge the progress I have made.
  9. Under armI can return to my own direction.

Take another slow breath. Notice what feels different now. You do not need the feeling to disappear. See if there is simply a little less pressure, or a little more choice in how you meet this moment.

Round 3: Choose what's next

  1. Side of handI am allowed to move at my pace.
  2. Top of headMy life is not a race against someone else.
  3. EyebrowI can celebrate others without abandoning myself.
  4. Side of eyeI choose goals that belong to me.
  5. Under eyeMy body can feel at home on my path.
  6. Under noseI trust the progress that is not always visible.
  7. ChinI am worthy in the middle of becoming.
  8. CollarboneI can take one step in my own direction.
  9. Under armMy attention is coming back to my life.
