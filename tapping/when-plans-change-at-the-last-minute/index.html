EFT tapping script: When Plans Change at the Last Minute

Use this session when a sudden change has left you disappointed, dysregulated, or struggling to shift away from the plan you expected.

Tap gently with two fingers, about seven times on each point. Say each phrase out loud or quietly in your head. There's no perfect way to do it.

Round 1: Say what's true

  1. Side of handThe plan changed and I do not like it.
  2. Top of headI had already prepared for something different.
  3. EyebrowI feel disappointed and thrown off.
  4. Side of eyeMy mind keeps returning to how it was supposed to go.
  5. Under eyeMy body is resisting this sudden change.
  6. Under noseI want the original plan back.
  7. ChinPart of me feels like the whole day is ruined.
  8. CollarboneI can admit that I feel frustrated.
  9. Under armIt is hard to shift gears this quickly.

Take one easy breath. Notice whether anything has shifted, even slightly. If nothing has changed yet, that is completely okay. Keep breathing, and we will continue.

Round 2: Soften it

  1. Side of handI can honor the disappointment and still adjust.
  2. Top of headThe original plan mattered to me.
  3. EyebrowI can release what is no longer available.
  4. Side of eyeI do not have to like the change to meet it.
  5. Under eyeMy body can find a new rhythm.
  6. Under noseI can ask what I need now.
  7. ChinA changed plan does not have to ruin the whole day.
  8. CollarboneI can give myself time to transition.
  9. Under armI can begin finding my footing again.

Take another slow breath. Notice what feels different now. You do not need the feeling to disappear. See if there is simply a little less pressure, or a little more choice in how you meet this moment.

Round 3: Choose what's next

  1. Side of handI can meet this version of the day.
  2. Top of headI am allowed to create a new plan.
  3. EyebrowI trust myself to adapt without rushing.
  4. Side of eyeI can notice what is still possible.
  5. Under eyeMy body can settle into the change.
  6. Under noseI can carry flexibility without abandoning my needs.
  7. ChinThis moment can unfold differently and still be okay.
  8. CollarboneI choose the next available step.
  9. Under armI am ready to move with what is here.
