EFT tapping script: Feeling Left Out

Use this session when being excluded, overlooked, or left out has stirred up hurt and made you question where you belong.

Tap gently with two fingers, about seven times on each point. Say each phrase out loud or quietly in your head. There's no perfect way to do it.

Round 1: Say what's true

  1. Side of handBeing left out really hurts.
  2. Top of headI keep wondering why I was not included.
  3. EyebrowMy mind is looking for what is wrong with me.
  4. Side of eyeI feel pushed to the outside.
  5. Under eyeI can feel this sting in my body.
  6. Under nosePart of me wants to pretend I do not care.
  7. ChinI am comparing myself to everyone who was there.
  8. CollarboneI can admit that I feel rejected.
  9. Under armThis moment is touching something tender.

Take one easy breath. Notice whether anything has shifted, even slightly. If nothing has changed yet, that is completely okay. Keep breathing, and we will continue.

Round 2: Soften it

  1. Side of handI can make room for the hurt without becoming it.
  2. Top of headI do not know the whole story.
  3. EyebrowBeing excluded does not define my value.
  4. Side of eyeI can stop searching for flaws in myself.
  5. Under eyeMy body can soften around this feeling.
  6. Under noseI am allowed to care and still protect my peace.
  7. ChinI can reach for people who make room for me.
  8. CollarboneBelonging does not depend on one invitation.
  9. Under armI can let this moment become smaller.

Take another slow breath. Notice what feels different now. You do not need the feeling to disappear. See if there is simply a little less pressure, or a little more choice in how you meet this moment.

Round 3: Choose what's next

  1. Side of handI am worthy of reciprocal connection.
  2. Top of headI do not have to earn my place by shrinking.
  3. EyebrowI can choose relationships that feel welcoming.
  4. Side of eyeI can be on my own side today.
  5. Under eyeMy body can remember that I belong.
  6. Under noseI am more than this one social moment.
  7. ChinI can let connection find me in other places.
  8. CollarboneI trust myself to move toward what feels genuine.
  9. Under armI carry my worth with me.
