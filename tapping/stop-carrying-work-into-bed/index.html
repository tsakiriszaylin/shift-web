EFT tapping script: Stop Carrying Work Into Bed

Use this session when unfinished tasks, emails, or decisions are following you into the night.

Tap gently with two fingers, about seven times on each point. Say each phrase out loud or quietly in your head. There's no perfect way to do it.

Round 1: Say what's true

  1. Side of handWork is still running through my mind.
  2. Top of headI keep thinking about what is unfinished.
  3. EyebrowI am worried I forgot something important.
  4. Side of eyePart of me is still at my desk.
  5. Under eyeMy body has not left work mode.
  6. Under noseI feel responsible for keeping everything moving.
  7. ChinIt is hard to stop when there is more to do.
  8. CollarboneI can admit I am still carrying work.
  9. Under armThis pressure followed me into bed.

Take one easy breath. Notice whether anything has shifted, even slightly. If nothing has changed yet, that’s completely okay. Keep breathing, and we’ll continue.

Round 2: Soften it

  1. Side of handThe workday is allowed to end.
  2. Top of headUnfinished does not mean unsafe.
  3. EyebrowI can write it down and return tomorrow.
  4. Side of eyeI do not need to be available every moment.
  5. Under eyeMy body can leave work mode now.
  6. Under noseRest will help me think more clearly.
  7. ChinI can care about my work without carrying it all night.
  8. CollarboneTomorrow has space for the next step.
  9. Under armI can put work down for now.

Take another slow breath. Notice what feels different now. You do not need the feeling to disappear. See if there is simply a little less pressure, or a little more choice in how you meet this moment.

Round 3: Choose what's next

  1. Side of handI am off the clock in this moment.
  2. Top of headMy value is bigger than my productivity.
  3. EyebrowI trust myself to return tomorrow.
  4. Side of eyeI can be present in my life outside work.
  5. Under eyeMy body is ready to rest.
  6. Under noseI have done enough for today.
  7. ChinBoundaries help me do sustainable work.
  8. CollarboneTonight belongs to me.
  9. Under armI am ready to sleep.
