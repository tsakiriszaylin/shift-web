EFT tapping script: Quieting Food Noise

Use this session when a craving feels loud and hard to ignore, and you want to meet it without judgment or restriction.

Tap gently with two fingers, about seven times on each point. Say each phrase out loud or quietly in your head. There's no perfect way to do it.

Round 1: Say what's true

  1. Side of handThis craving feels loud right now.
  2. Top of headMy mind keeps circling back to food.
  3. EyebrowPart of me feels like I have to act on this right now.
  4. Side of eyeI notice how persistent this urge feels.
  5. Under eyeMy body feels restless with this craving.
  6. Under noseI judge myself for wanting this.
  7. ChinThis noise feels hard to think past.
  8. CollarboneI can admit this urge feels strong.
  9. Under armWanting this doesn’t feel like a choice right now.

Take one easy breath. Notice whether anything has shifted, even slightly. If nothing has changed yet, that’s completely okay. Keep breathing, and we’ll continue.

Round 2: Soften it

  1. Side of handI can notice this craving without judging myself for it.
  2. Top of headThis urge doesn’t have to control what I do next.
  3. EyebrowI don’t have to act on this feeling right away.
  4. Side of eyeI can let this craving be loud without believing everything it says.
  5. Under eyeMy body can settle, even while this urge is here.
  6. Under noseWanting something doesn’t make me weak.
  7. ChinI can make space between the urge and what I do.
  8. CollarboneI’m allowed to choose, even when the pull feels strong.
  9. Under armThis noise can get quieter the more I let it just be.

Take another slow breath. Notice what feels different now. You don’t need the feeling to disappear. See if there’s simply a little less pressure, or a little more choice in how you meet this moment.

Round 3: Choose what's next

  1. Side of handI’m capable of listening to my body without judgment.
  2. Top of headI trust myself to know what I actually need.
  3. EyebrowI’m allowed to feel satisfied, not just full.
  4. Side of eyeI can meet this craving with curiosity instead of guilt.
  5. Under eyeMy body deserves patience, not punishment.
  6. Under noseI’m learning to hear myself over the noise.
  7. ChinI can be gentle with myself in this moment.
  8. CollarboneI’m allowed to take up space and take care of myself.
  9. Under armI’m ready to feel calmer around food.
