EFT tapping script: Overwhelmed by My To-Do List

Use this session when your responsibilities feel tangled together and the pressure is making it difficult to begin.

Tap gently with two fingers, about seven times on each point. Say each phrase out loud or quietly in your head. There's no perfect way to do it.

Round 1: Say what's true

  1. Side of handThere is too much to do.
  2. Top of headEverything feels urgent.
  3. EyebrowI do not know where to start.
  4. Side of eyeMy mind keeps jumping between tasks.
  5. Under eyeI can feel this pressure in my body.
  6. Under noseI am afraid I will fall behind.
  7. ChinI am judging myself for feeling stuck.
  8. CollarboneI can admit I feel overwhelmed.
  9. Under armI cannot carry everything at once.

Take one easy breath. Notice whether anything has shifted, even slightly. If nothing has changed yet, that’s completely okay. Keep breathing, and we’ll continue.

Round 2: Soften it

  1. Side of handI can pause before I choose.
  2. Top of headNot everything is equally urgent.
  3. EyebrowI only need one next step.
  4. Side of eyeI can let the other tasks wait their turn.
  5. Under eyeMy body is allowed to soften.
  6. Under noseProgress can be small and still count.
  7. ChinFeeling overwhelmed does not mean I am failing.
  8. CollarboneI can choose what matters most right now.
  9. Under armI can put down the rest for a moment.

Take another slow breath. Notice what feels different now. You do not need the feeling to disappear. See if there is simply a little less pressure, or a little more choice in how you meet this moment.

Round 3: Choose what's next

  1. Side of handI am ready to begin one thing.
  2. Top of headI can work at a human pace.
  3. EyebrowI trust myself to choose the next step.
  4. Side of eyeI can return when my mind wanders.
  5. Under eyeMy attention can settle here.
  6. Under noseI do not need to finish everything today.
  7. ChinOne completed step creates momentum.
  8. CollarboneI can be focused without being frantic.
  9. Under armI know what I will do next.
