EFT tapping script: Checking Your Bank Account Before Payday

Use this session when you're anxious about checking your bank account and money stress is creeping into everything else.

Tap gently with two fingers, about seven times on each point. Say each phrase out loud or quietly in your head. There's no perfect way to do it.

Round 1: Say what's true

  1. Side of handI am nervous to check my bank account.
  2. Top of headMy mind keeps calculating what is left.
  3. EyebrowI am afraid of what the number will say.
  4. Side of eyeI keep comparing this to where I think I should be.
  5. Under eyeMy body tenses up thinking about money.
  6. Under noseI want to feel secure instead of anxious.
  7. ChinPart of me feels like this is my fault.
  8. CollarboneI can admit that money stress is wearing on me.
  9. Under armThis worry is following me through the day.

Take one easy breath. Notice whether anything has shifted, even slightly. If nothing has changed yet, that is completely okay. Keep breathing, and we will continue.

Round 2: Soften it

  1. Side of handI can look at the number without it defining me.
  2. Top of headA number in an account is not my worth.
  3. EyebrowI can make a plan instead of just worrying.
  4. Side of eyeI do not have to compare my situation to anyone else's.
  5. Under eyeMy body can release some of this tension.
  6. Under noseI am doing the best I can with what I have.
  7. ChinThis is a circumstance, not a character flaw.
  8. CollarboneI can face this honestly, one step at a time.
  9. Under armI can let some of this pressure go.

Take another slow breath. Notice what feels different now. You do not need the feeling to disappear. See if there is simply a little less pressure, or a little more choice in how you meet this moment.

Round 3: Choose what's next

  1. Side of handI am capable of handling my finances step by step.
  2. Top of headI trust myself to make thoughtful choices.
  3. EyebrowI can face payday without dread.
  4. Side of eyeI am allowed to feel secure even while things are tight.
  5. Under eyeMy body can settle around this.
  6. Under noseI am building toward something steadier.
  7. ChinMy worth is not tied to a number.
  8. CollarboneI can take the next useful step.
  9. Under armI am ready to look at this clearly.
