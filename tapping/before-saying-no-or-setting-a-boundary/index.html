EFT tapping script: Before Saying No or Setting a Boundary

Use this session before declining a request or naming a limit when guilt, fear of conflict, or concern about disappointing someone is making you hesitate.

Tap gently with two fingers, about seven times on each point. Say each phrase out loud or quietly in your head. There's no perfect way to do it.

Round 1: Say what's true

  1. Side of handI know I need a boundary, and I feel uncomfortable.
  2. Top of headI am worried they will be disappointed in me.
  3. EyebrowPart of me wants to say yes just to avoid tension.
  4. Side of eyeI keep rehearsing every possible reaction.
  5. Under eyeMy body is bracing for conflict.
  6. Under noseI feel guilty for having limits.
  7. ChinI am afraid this boundary will change the relationship.
  8. CollarboneI can admit that saying no feels vulnerable.
  9. Under armI am carrying their reaction before it has happened.

Take one easy breath. Notice whether anything has shifted, even slightly. If nothing has changed yet, that is completely okay. Keep breathing, and we will continue.

Round 2: Soften it

  1. Side of handI can tolerate the discomfort of being clear.
  2. Top of headA boundary is information about what I can offer.
  3. EyebrowI do not need a dramatic explanation.
  4. Side of eyeTheir feelings can exist without deciding my answer.
  5. Under eyeMy body can soften while I hold my limit.
  6. Under noseGuilt does not always mean I am doing something wrong.
  7. ChinI can be kind without saying yes.
  8. CollarboneA healthy relationship can make room for honesty.
  9. Under armI can release responsibility for managing the response.

Take another slow breath. Notice what feels different now. You do not need the feeling to disappear. See if there is simply a little less pressure, or a little more choice in how you meet this moment.

Round 3: Choose what's next

  1. Side of handI am ready to communicate my boundary clearly.
  2. Top of headI can use simple and respectful words.
  3. EyebrowI trust myself to stay connected to what I need.
  4. Side of eyeI can listen without negotiating against myself.
  5. Under eyeMy body can feel calm and firm.
  6. Under noseI am allowed to protect my capacity.
  7. ChinI can repeat my answer if clarity is needed.
  8. CollarboneI can let honesty support this relationship.
  9. Under armI am ready to say what is true for me.
