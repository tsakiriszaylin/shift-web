EFT tapping script: When Money Anxiety Spikes

Use this session when a bill, balance, expense, or financial unknown has triggered panic and made it difficult to think about money clearly.

Tap gently with two fingers, about seven times on each point. Say each phrase out loud or quietly in your head. There's no perfect way to do it.

Round 1: Say what's true

  1. Side of handMoney anxiety is taking over right now.
  2. Top of headMy mind keeps jumping to the worst financial outcome.
  3. EyebrowI feel ashamed that this is affecting me so much.
  4. Side of eyeI want to avoid looking at the numbers.
  5. Under eyeMy body is reacting like there is immediate danger.
  6. Under noseI feel pressure to solve everything at once.
  7. ChinPart of me believes I should have handled this better.
  8. CollarboneI can admit that I feel scared.
  9. Under armThis financial stress is taking up so much space.

Take one easy breath. Notice whether anything has shifted, even slightly. If nothing has changed yet, that is completely okay. Keep breathing, and we will continue.

Round 2: Soften it

  1. Side of handI can settle before I look at the next step.
  2. Top of headA frightening thought is not the same as a number.
  3. EyebrowI can face one piece of information at a time.
  4. Side of eyeAvoiding the situation is understandable, but not required.
  5. Under eyeMy body can come out of immediate alarm.
  6. Under noseI do not have to solve the entire future today.
  7. ChinShame will not help me see the situation clearly.
  8. CollarboneI can ask for information or support if I need it.
  9. Under armI can make this moment smaller and more specific.

Take another slow breath. Notice what feels different now. You do not need the feeling to disappear. See if there is simply a little less pressure, or a little more choice in how you meet this moment.

Round 3: Choose what's next

  1. Side of handI am ready to look at one practical next step.
  2. Top of headI can work with facts instead of imagined outcomes.
  3. EyebrowI trust myself to make one grounded choice.
  4. Side of eyeI can pause again if the anxiety rises.
  5. Under eyeMy body can stay steady while I look at money.
  6. Under noseMy financial situation does not define my worth.
  7. ChinI can seek appropriate guidance when I need it.
  8. CollarboneSmall actions can create more clarity.
  9. Under armI am ready to begin with what is in front of me.
