EFT tapping script: Dreading the Day Before It Starts

Use this session when you wake up already anticipating everything difficult that might happen today.

Tap gently with two fingers, about seven times on each point. Say each phrase out loud or quietly in your head. There's no perfect way to do it.

Round 1: Say what's true

  1. Side of handI am already dreading this day.
  2. Top of headEverything ahead feels heavy.
  3. EyebrowI wish I could avoid what is coming.
  4. Side of eyeMy mind is running through the entire day.
  5. Under eyeMy body feels tense before I have even started.
  6. Under noseI do not feel ready for this.
  7. ChinI am judging myself for not feeling motivated.
  8. CollarboneI can admit I want to stay here.
  9. Under armThe whole day feels like too much.

Take one easy breath. Notice whether anything has shifted, even slightly. If nothing has changed yet, that’s completely okay. Keep breathing, and we’ll continue.

Round 2: Soften it

  1. Side of handI do not have to live the whole day right now.
  2. Top of headI only need to meet this morning.
  3. EyebrowI can take the first step before the next one.
  4. Side of eyeThe day may be different from what I imagine.
  5. Under eyeMy body can soften before I begin.
  6. Under noseI can move without feeling fully ready.
  7. ChinA hard morning does not decide the whole day.
  8. CollarboneI can give myself a gentler start.
  9. Under armI can make the day smaller.

Take another slow breath. Notice what feels different now. You do not need the feeling to disappear. See if there is simply a little less pressure, or a little more choice in how you meet this moment.

Round 3: Choose what's next

  1. Side of handI am ready for the next ten minutes.
  2. Top of headI can meet one moment at a time.
  3. EyebrowI trust myself to adapt as the day unfolds.
  4. Side of eyeI can notice what goes better than expected.
  5. Under eyeMy body can move with steadiness.
  6. Under noseI do not need perfect motivation to begin.
  7. ChinI have handled difficult days before.
  8. CollarboneI can bring support with me.
  9. Under armI am ready to take the first step.
