EFT tapping script: Walking Into a Room Where You Know No One

Use this session right before entering a space—a party, a new job, a networking event—where you don't know anyone yet.

Tap gently with two fingers, about seven times on each point. Say each phrase out loud or quietly in your head. There's no perfect way to do it.

Round 1: Say what's true

  1. Side of handI am about to walk into a room where I know no one.
  2. Top of headMy mind is imagining every awkward possibility.
  3. EyebrowI feel nervous about being on my own here.
  4. Side of eyeI keep scanning for a familiar face that is not there.
  5. Under eyeMy body feels tense before I even walk in.
  6. Under noseI want to feel confident instead of exposed.
  7. ChinPart of me wants to turn around and leave.
  8. CollarboneI can admit that this feels intimidating.
  9. Under armWalking in alone feels like a lot right now.

Take one easy breath. Notice whether anything has shifted, even slightly. If nothing has changed yet, that is completely okay. Keep breathing, and we will continue.

Round 2: Soften it

  1. Side of handI can walk in without needing to know everyone.
  2. Top of headMost people here feel a little nervous too.
  3. EyebrowI do not have to perform, just be present.
  4. Side of eyeI can find one person or one moment to anchor to.
  5. Under eyeMy body can settle as I move through the room.
  6. Under noseI am allowed to ease in slowly.
  7. ChinBeing new here does not make me out of place.
  8. CollarboneI can be myself, even among strangers.
  9. Under armI can let this feel less big than it seems.

Take another slow breath. Notice what feels different now. You do not need the feeling to disappear. See if there is simply a little less pressure, or a little more choice in how you meet this moment.

Round 3: Choose what's next

  1. Side of handI am capable of walking into this room.
  2. Top of headMy mind can stay steady, even here.
  3. EyebrowI trust myself to find connection.
  4. Side of eyeI can be curious instead of afraid.
  5. Under eyeMy body can feel grounded in this space.
  6. Under noseI belong wherever I choose to show up.
  7. ChinI do not need to know everyone to feel okay.
  8. CollarboneI can carry my own steadiness into this room.
  9. Under armI am ready to walk in.
