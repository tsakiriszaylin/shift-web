EFT tapping script: Googling Symptoms at 2AM

Use this session when a late-night search spiral has convinced you something is seriously wrong and anxiety has taken over.

Tap gently with two fingers, about seven times on each point. Say each phrase out loud or quietly in your head. There's no perfect way to do it.

Round 1: Say what's true

  1. Side of handI’ve been searching my symptoms and I feel scared.
  2. Top of headMy mind has jumped to the worst possibility.
  3. EyebrowI keep reading things that make it worse.
  4. Side of eyeI’m convinced something is seriously wrong.
  5. Under eyeMy body feels alarmed by what I’ve read.
  6. Under noseI want certainty and I can’t find it online.
  7. ChinPart of me can’t stop searching for more.
  8. CollarboneI can admit that this spiral has taken over.
  9. Under armIt’s late and this fear feels very loud.

Take one easy breath. Notice whether anything has shifted, even slightly. If nothing has changed yet, that’s completely okay. Keep breathing, and we’ll continue.

Round 2: Soften it

  1. Side of handI can close this tab and come back to now.
  2. Top of headA search result isn’t a diagnosis.
  3. EyebrowI can separate what I’m reading from what I actually know.
  4. Side of eyeI don’t have to find every possibility tonight.
  5. Under eyeMy body can settle even without certainty.
  6. Under noseI can get real answers from a real person if I need them.
  7. ChinFear is loud right now, but it isn’t the whole truth.
  8. CollarboneI can be gentle with myself in this moment.
  9. Under armI can set this worry down until morning.

Take another slow breath. Notice what feels different now. You don’t need the feeling to disappear. See if there’s simply a little less pressure, or a little more choice in how you meet this moment.

Round 3: Choose what's next

  1. Side of handI trust my body more than a search result.
  2. Top of headI can seek real care instead of spiraling alone.
  3. EyebrowI’m allowed to feel calm again.
  4. Side of eyeI can focus on the facts I actually have.
  5. Under eyeMy body can rest even with unanswered questions.
  6. Under noseI don’t have to solve this at 2am.
  7. ChinI’m capable of handling whatever this turns out to be.
  8. CollarboneI can put the phone down now.
  9. Under armI’m ready to let this rest until I can act on it.
