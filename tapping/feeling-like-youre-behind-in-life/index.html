EFT tapping script: Feeling Like You're Behind in Life

Use this session when comparing your milestones to everyone else's is making you feel like you're falling behind in life.

Tap gently with two fingers, about seven times on each point. Say each phrase out loud or quietly in your head. There's no perfect way to do it.

Round 1: Say what's true

  1. Side of handI feel like I am behind in life.
  2. Top of headI keep measuring myself against a timeline.
  3. EyebrowI am comparing my life to everyone else's milestones.
  4. Side of eyeI notice what I have not accomplished yet.
  5. Under eyeMy body feels the pressure of this comparison.
  6. Under noseI want to feel like I am where I should be.
  7. ChinPart of me believes I am running out of time.
  8. CollarboneI can admit that this feeling is heavy.
  9. Under armThis timeline is weighing on me.

Take one easy breath. Notice whether anything has shifted, even slightly. If nothing has changed yet, that is completely okay. Keep breathing, and we will continue.

Round 2: Soften it

  1. Side of handI can question where this timeline even came from.
  2. Top of headThere is no single right pace for a life.
  3. EyebrowI can notice my own progress instead of theirs.
  4. Side of eyeI do not have to measure myself against a highlight reel.
  5. Under eyeMy body can soften around this pressure.
  6. Under noseI am allowed to be exactly where I am.
  7. ChinThere is still time for whatever matters to me.
  8. CollarboneI can release the idea that I am late.
  9. Under armI can let this timeline loosen its grip.

Take another slow breath. Notice what feels different now. You do not need the feeling to disappear. See if there is simply a little less pressure, or a little more choice in how you meet this moment.

Round 3: Choose what's next

  1. Side of handI trust my own pace.
  2. Top of headMy life is not a race against anyone else's.
  3. EyebrowI can build a life that fits me, not a schedule.
  4. Side of eyeI can celebrate my own progress.
  5. Under eyeMy body can feel at ease with where I am.
  6. Under noseI am exactly on time for my own life.
  7. ChinI am allowed to take the time I need.
  8. CollarboneI can move forward without rushing.
  9. Under armI am ready to release this comparison.
