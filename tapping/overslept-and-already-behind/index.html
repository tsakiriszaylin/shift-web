EFT tapping script: Overslept and Already Behind

Use this session when you've woken up late and the whole morning already feels rushed, chaotic, and behind.

Tap gently with two fingers, about seven times on each point. Say each phrase out loud or quietly in your head. There's no perfect way to do it.

Round 1: Say what's true

  1. Side of handI overslept and I’m already behind.
  2. Top of headMy mind is racing through everything I need to do.
  3. EyebrowI feel panicked about being late.
  4. Side of eyeI keep checking the clock and feeling worse.
  5. Under eyeMy body feels rushed and tense.
  6. Under noseI want to catch up immediately.
  7. ChinPart of me is already dreading how the day will go.
  8. CollarboneI can admit that this morning feels chaotic.
  9. Under armEverything feels urgent right now.

Take one easy breath. Notice whether anything has shifted, even slightly. If nothing has changed yet, that’s completely okay. Keep breathing, and we’ll continue.

Round 2: Soften it

  1. Side of handI can slow down even while I move quickly.
  2. Top of headRushing internally will not make me faster.
  3. EyebrowA late start doesn’t ruin the whole day.
  4. Side of eyeI can focus on the next single step.
  5. Under eyeMy body can move with less panic.
  6. Under noseI can catch up without losing my composure.
  7. ChinOne rushed morning isn’t a disaster.
  8. CollarboneI can breathe even while I hurry.
  9. Under armI can let the panic settle a little.

Take another slow breath. Notice what feels different now. You don’t need the feeling to disappear. See if there’s simply a little less pressure, or a little more choice in how you meet this moment.

Round 3: Choose what's next

  1. Side of handI trust myself to handle a rushed morning.
  2. Top of headI can keep a clear mind, even under pressure.
  3. EyebrowI can recover this day.
  4. Side of eyeI can move efficiently without spiraling.
  5. Under eyeMy body can find its rhythm again.
  6. Under noseI’m capable of catching up calmly.
  7. ChinThis morning doesn’t decide the rest of my day.
  8. CollarboneI can reset, even mid-rush.
  9. Under armI’m ready to move forward steadily.
