EFT tapping script: Prepare for a Difficult Conversation

Use this session when you are avoiding a conversation, worried about saying the wrong thing, or putting pressure on yourself to control how it goes.

Tap gently with two fingers, about seven times on each point. Say each phrase out loud or quietly in your head. There's no perfect way to do it.

Round 1: Say what's true

  1. Side of handI feel nervous about this conversation.
  2. Top of headI am worried about saying the wrong thing.
  3. EyebrowPart of me wants to avoid it.
  4. Side of eyeI keep rehearsing every possible response.
  5. Under eyeMy body is bracing for conflict.
  6. Under noseI want to be understood.
  7. ChinI cannot control how they react.
  8. CollarboneI can admit this feels hard.
  9. Under armThis is a lot to hold alone.

Take one easy breath. Notice whether anything has shifted, even slightly. If nothing has changed yet, that’s completely okay. Keep breathing, and we’ll continue.

Round 2: Soften it

  1. Side of handI can make room for these nerves.
  2. Top of headI do not need a perfect script.
  3. EyebrowI can slow down before I respond.
  4. Side of eyeI can listen without abandoning myself.
  5. Under eyeMy body is allowed to soften.
  6. Under noseI can speak one honest sentence at a time.
  7. ChinTheir reaction is not mine to manage.
  8. CollarboneI can stay connected to what matters.
  9. Under armI can carry less pressure into this talk.

Take another slow breath. Notice what feels different now. You do not need the feeling to disappear. See if there is simply a little less pressure, or a little more choice in how you meet this moment.

Round 3: Choose what's next

  1. Side of handI am ready to have this conversation.
  2. Top of headI can be clear and kind.
  3. EyebrowI trust myself to find the words.
  4. Side of eyeI can pause when I need to.
  5. Under eyeI can stay grounded in my body.
  6. Under noseMy needs deserve a voice.
  7. ChinI can handle an imperfect moment.
  8. CollarboneI can respect both of us.
  9. Under armI am ready to begin.
