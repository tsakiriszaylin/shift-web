EFT tapping script: Procrastinating Because It Feels Too Big

Use this session when a task feels so large, important, or unclear that you keep avoiding the first step.

Tap gently with two fingers, about seven times on each point. Say each phrase out loud or quietly in your head. There's no perfect way to do it.

Round 1: Say what's true

  1. Side of handThis task feels too big to begin.
  2. Top of headI keep putting it off and feeling worse.
  3. EyebrowI do not know where to start.
  4. Side of eyeI am imagining everything the task requires.
  5. Under eyeMy body feels stuck under the pressure.
  6. Under noseI am afraid I will not do it well.
  7. ChinPart of me is waiting to feel ready.
  8. CollarboneI can admit that I am avoiding this.
  9. Under armThe whole project is crowding this one moment.

Take one easy breath. Notice whether anything has shifted, even slightly. If nothing has changed yet, that is completely okay. Keep breathing, and we will continue.

Round 2: Soften it

  1. Side of handI can make the task smaller.
  2. Top of headI do not need to finish in order to begin.
  3. EyebrowI can choose one visible next step.
  4. Side of eyeAn imperfect start still creates movement.
  5. Under eyeMy body can soften before I work.
  6. Under noseI am allowed to learn as I go.
  7. ChinReadiness can come after I begin.
  8. CollarboneI can work for a few minutes and reassess.
  9. Under armI can put down the rest of the project for now.

Take another slow breath. Notice what feels different now. You do not need the feeling to disappear. See if there is simply a little less pressure, or a little more choice in how you meet this moment.

Round 3: Choose what's next

  1. Side of handI am ready for one manageable action.
  2. Top of headI can begin before I feel perfectly confident.
  3. EyebrowI trust small steps to create momentum.
  4. Side of eyeI can focus on what is directly in front of me.
  5. Under eyeMy attention can settle on this step.
  6. Under noseI do not need perfection to make progress.
  7. ChinStarting is enough for this moment.
  8. CollarboneI can support myself while I work.
  9. Under armI know what I will do first.
