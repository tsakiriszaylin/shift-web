EFT tapping script: Being Okay With Being Alone

Use this session when being alone feels heavier than it should and you want to find peace in your own company.

Tap gently with two fingers, about seven times on each point. Say each phrase out loud or quietly in your head. There's no perfect way to do it.

Round 1: Say what's true

  1. Side of handBeing alone right now feels harder than I expected.
  2. Top of headMy mind keeps comparing this to being with someone.
  3. EyebrowPart of me feels like being alone means something is missing.
  4. Side of eyeI notice how loud the quiet feels.
  5. Under eyeMy body feels the weight of this solitude.
  6. Under noseI wish someone were here with me right now.
  7. ChinPart of me feels a little embarrassed to be alone.
  8. CollarboneI can admit that this loneliness is real.
  9. Under armThis feeling is uncomfortable, and that’s okay to say.

Take one easy breath. Notice whether anything has shifted, even slightly. If nothing has changed yet, that’s completely okay. Keep breathing, and we’ll continue.

Round 2: Soften it

  1. Side of handI can be alone without it meaning something is wrong.
  2. Top of headMy own company is allowed to be enough.
  3. EyebrowI don’t have to fill this quiet to feel okay.
  4. Side of eyeI can let this stillness be peaceful instead of empty.
  5. Under eyeMy body can relax into this time alone.
  6. Under noseI’m allowed to enjoy my own presence.
  7. ChinI can be good company for myself.
  8. CollarboneI’m allowed to feel whole on my own.
  9. Under armThis time alone can be restful, not just lonely.

Take another slow breath. Notice what feels different now. You don’t need the feeling to disappear. See if there’s simply a little less pressure, or a little more choice in how you meet this moment.

Round 3: Choose what's next

  1. Side of handI’m capable of enjoying my own company.
  2. Top of headI trust that I’m not missing out by being here with myself.
  3. EyebrowI’m allowed to feel complete on my own.
  4. Side of eyeI can find comfort in this quiet.
  5. Under eyeMy body feels at ease being alone tonight.
  6. Under noseI’m learning to enjoy time with just myself.
  7. ChinI can be my own steady presence.
  8. CollarboneI’m allowed to feel at peace, alone or not.
  9. Under armI’m ready to let tonight be enough, just as it is.
