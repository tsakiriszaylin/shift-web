EFT tapping script: Waiting for News and Assuming the Worst

Use this session when you are waiting for an answer, result, or decision and your mind keeps jumping to the worst possible outcome.

Tap gently with two fingers, about seven times on each point. Say each phrase out loud or quietly in your head. There's no perfect way to do it.

Round 1: Say what's true

  1. Side of handI am waiting for an answer I cannot control.
  2. Top of headMy mind keeps jumping to the worst outcome.
  3. EyebrowThe uncertainty feels hard to carry.
  4. Side of eyeI keep checking for news.
  5. Under eyeMy body is bracing for something bad.
  6. Under noseI want certainty right now.
  7. ChinPart of me believes worrying will prepare me.
  8. CollarboneI can admit that I feel afraid.
  9. Under armThis waiting is taking up so much space.

Take one easy breath. Notice whether anything has shifted, even slightly. If nothing has changed yet, that is completely okay. Keep breathing, and we will continue.

Round 2: Soften it

  1. Side of handI can make room for not knowing yet.
  2. Top of headA frightening thought is not a prediction.
  3. EyebrowI can pause before checking again.
  4. Side of eyeThere may be outcomes I have not imagined.
  5. Under eyeMy body can soften while I wait.
  6. Under noseI do not need certainty to take this breath.
  7. ChinWorrying cannot control the answer.
  8. CollarboneI can support myself through uncertainty.
  9. Under armI can return to the moment in front of me.

Take another slow breath. Notice what feels different now. You do not need the feeling to disappear. See if there is simply a little less pressure, or a little more choice in how you meet this moment.

Round 3: Choose what's next

  1. Side of handI can handle the answer when it arrives.
  2. Top of headI choose facts over imagined outcomes.
  3. EyebrowI can let the waiting exist in the background.
  4. Side of eyeMy attention belongs to me right now.
  5. Under eyeMy body is safe in this moment.
  6. Under noseI trust myself to take the next step.
  7. ChinI do not have to rehearse every possibility.
  8. CollarboneI can stay grounded without knowing.
  9. Under armFor now, I am here and I am okay.
