EFT tapping script: Scared of Going to the Gym

Use this session before heading to the gym when the thought of walking in feels more intimidating than the workout itself.

Tap gently with two fingers, about seven times on each point. Say each phrase out loud or quietly in your head. There's no perfect way to do it.

Round 1: Say what's true

  1. Side of handI feel nervous about going to the gym.
  2. Top of headI’m scared people will notice or judge me.
  3. EyebrowPart of me feels like I don’t belong there yet.
  4. Side of eyeI keep imagining doing something wrong.
  5. Under eyeMy body feels tense just thinking about walking in.
  6. Under noseI compare myself to everyone else there.
  7. ChinPart of me wants to just stay home instead.
  8. CollarboneI can admit how intimidating this feels.
  9. Under armThis fear feels bigger than the workout itself.

Take one easy breath. Notice whether anything has shifted, even slightly. If nothing has changed yet, that’s completely okay. Keep breathing, and we’ll continue.

Round 2: Soften it

  1. Side of handI can feel nervous and still walk in anyway.
  2. Top of headEveryone there started as a beginner once.
  3. EyebrowI don’t have to look a certain way to belong there.
  4. Side of eyeMost people are focused on themselves, not me.
  5. Under eyeMy body can settle a little, even with these nerves.
  6. Under noseI’m allowed to not know what I’m doing yet.
  7. ChinI can go at my own pace without comparing.
  8. CollarboneI’m allowed to take up space while I learn.
  9. Under armThis gets easier every time I show up.

Take another slow breath. Notice what feels different now. You don’t need the feeling to disappear. See if there’s simply a little less pressure, or a little more choice in how you meet this moment.

Round 3: Choose what's next

  1. Side of handI’m capable of walking in, even nervous.
  2. Top of headI trust myself to figure it out as I go.
  3. EyebrowI’m allowed to be a beginner here.
  4. Side of eyeI can focus on myself and my own progress.
  5. Under eyeMy body is capable of more than I give it credit for.
  6. Under noseI’m building confidence one visit at a time.
  7. ChinI can be proud of myself just for showing up.
  8. CollarboneI’m allowed to belong here as much as anyone else.
  9. Under armI’m ready to walk in and start.
