EFT tapping script: Feeling Like You're Always the One Who Reaches Out

Use this session when you've realized you're always the one initiating contact and it's starting to feel lonely or discouraging.

Tap gently with two fingers, about seven times on each point. Say each phrase out loud or quietly in your head. There's no perfect way to do it.

Round 1: Say what's true

  1. Side of handI always seem to be the one who reaches out.
  2. Top of headI keep noticing who initiates and who doesn’t.
  3. EyebrowI’m wondering if they would reach out at all.
  4. Side of eyeI feel hurt by the imbalance.
  5. Under eyeMy body feels tired of carrying the effort.
  6. Under noseI want to matter to them the way they matter to me.
  7. ChinPart of me is afraid to stop and see what happens.
  8. CollarboneI can admit that this feels lonely.
  9. Under armThis pattern is starting to wear on me.

Take one easy breath. Notice whether anything has shifted, even slightly. If nothing has changed yet, that’s completely okay. Keep breathing, and we’ll continue.

Round 2: Soften it

  1. Side of handI can notice this pattern without deciding I’m unwanted.
  2. Top of headPeople show love in different ways and paces.
  3. EyebrowI can ask for more without assuming the worst.
  4. Side of eyeI don’t have to keep score to know how I feel.
  5. Under eyeMy body can release the pressure of always initiating.
  6. Under noseI’m allowed to take a step back and rest.
  7. ChinMy effort has value, even if it goes unmatched sometimes.
  8. CollarboneI can be honest about what I need from this relationship.
  9. Under armI can let myself see clearly what’s really here.

Take another slow breath. Notice what feels different now. You don’t need the feeling to disappear. See if there’s simply a little less pressure, or a little more choice in how you meet this moment.

Round 3: Choose what's next

  1. Side of handI’m worthy of being reached for, not just reaching.
  2. Top of headI trust myself to notice what relationships give back.
  3. EyebrowI can choose where to put my effort.
  4. Side of eyeI can value connection without over-giving.
  5. Under eyeMy body can feel steady either way.
  6. Under noseI can speak up about what I need.
  7. ChinI deserve relationships that reach back.
  8. CollarboneI’m allowed to protect my own energy.
  9. Under armI’m ready to see this clearly.
